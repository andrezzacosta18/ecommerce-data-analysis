# Day 1 — Business Understanding & Database Design

## Step 1 — Business Understanding

### Business Scenario

An e-commerce company wants to better understand its sales performance.

Management has noticed changes in sales results over time and wants to use the available data to understand whether the company's performance is improving or declining.

The company also wants to identify the factors that may be influencing these results.

### Business Problem

**How are the company's sales performing, and what factors are influencing the results?**

### Analysis Objective

Analyze sales data to identify patterns, trends, and changes in performance that can support business decision-making.
